A confident voice is not proof of identity.

Voice phishing works because phone calls carry no proof. A caller gives a familiar name, sounds like they belong, and asks for something reasonable. The person answering has nothing to check against.

Staff Verify adds that check. It takes a few seconds, it does not rely on recognising a voice, and it gives the person answering a clear reason to pause when the code is wrong.

  • IT support“I need to reset your password or approve a sign-in.”
  • Finance“Can you change these payment details today?”
  • A colleague“Send me that file, I can't get into my email.”

One phone call, three steps.

  1. The caller reads out their code

    They open Staff Verify and say the six digits on screen. The code is replaced every 60 seconds.

  2. You find them by name

    Search the staff list in your own app and enter the code you were given.

  3. The app answers straight away

    A match confirms the caller is who they say they are. No match means stop and check another way.

What is in the app.

Built to be opened in the middle of a phone call, so there is very little to learn.

Codes that expire in a minute
Each signed-in person gets a fresh six-digit code every 60 seconds, so a code that is overheard or written down soon stops working.
Instant check by name
Search for a member of staff, enter their code and get a clear answer on the spot.
Microsoft sign-in
Staff sign in with their Microsoft (Azure AD) work account. Accounts are set up by your administrator.
Biometric unlock
The app opens with the phone's own biometric unlock, so it is quick to reach during a call.
Your organisation's branding
The interface carries your organisation's identity, so staff recognise it as their own tool.

Closed by design.

  • Invitation only

    It is an internal tool. Nobody can use it without an account provisioned by an administrator.

  • Nothing shared with third parties

    The app does not share user data with other companies or organisations.

  • Encrypted in transit

    Data moving between the app and the service is encrypted.

Data handling as declared in the app's Google Play data safety section.

Available now.

Staff Verify is published on Google Play for Android, with a web sign-in at staffverify.me. Organisations are enrolled by the developer, and staff accounts are then provisioned by an administrator.
